M S Seo(이하 "개발자")는 무료 앱 Gender reveal(이하 "앱")을 제공하며, 이용자의 개인정보를 소중히 다룹니다. 이 문서는 앱이 어떤 정보를 수집·이용하는지 설명합니다.

1. 기기에만 저장되는 정보

앱에 입력하는 아기 성별과 출산 예정일은 이용자의 기기 안에만 저장되며, 개발자나 외부 서버로 전송되지 않습니다. 앱을 삭제하면 함께 삭제됩니다.

아기 편지 알림은 기기 안에서 예약되는 로컬 알림이며, 알림을 위해 서버로 정보를 보내지 않습니다.

2. 제3자 서비스가 수집하는 정보

앱은 아래 제3자 서비스를 사용하며, 이 서비스들이 자동으로 정보를 수집할 수 있습니다. 개발자는 이름, 연락처 등 이용자를 직접 식별하는 정보를 수집하지 않습니다.

Gender reveal Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 (previous version: September 11, 2023)

M S Seo ("we") provides Gender reveal (the "App") as a free app. This page explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

1. Information stored only on your device

The baby's gender and due date you enter are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to any server. They are removed when you delete the App.

Baby letter notifications are scheduled locally on your device; no information is sent to a server for them.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information automatically. We do not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or contact details.

ServiceData collectedPurpose
Firebase Crashlytics (Google)Crash logs, device model, OS version, app version, installation identifierDiagnosing errors and improving stability
Firebase Analytics (Google)App usage events (screens viewed, buttons used), app instance ID, device information, approximate location (country level)Usage statistics and product improvement
Google AdMobAdvertising ID (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA, if permitted), device information, IP addressServing ads and measuring ad performance

The due date and baby's gender are never sent to these services.

Privacy policies of these services: Google Privacy Policy, Privacy and Security in Firebase, AdMob privacy

3. International data transfer

Information collected by the services above is processed by Google LLC on servers in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Crashlytics crash records are kept for 90 days and Analytics event data according to the Google Analytics retention setting (up to 14 months); other data is retained according to Google's policies.

4. Advertising choices

Users in the EEA and the UK are shown a consent form when they open the App, and their choices determine whether personalized ads and analytics are used. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been provided, we will delete it immediately.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information. Since we do not hold information that identifies you, data on your device can be removed by deleting the App, and we will help with requests regarding data held by third-party services.

7. Security

All transmissions use encrypted connections (HTTPS). However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes to this policy
